### Choose Your Challenge
Build a working proof of concept within one or more of these tracks:

1. Stability & Social Cohesion: Reduce everyday friction, resolve local disputes, improve crisis coordination, or enable trusted communication between communities.

2. Transparency & Accountability: Help people understand policies, track public spending, report issues, access services, and scrutinize institutional decisions.

3. Safety, Reporting & Protection: Enable people to safely report threats, violence, or abuse and access clear pathways to timely support and protection.

Cross-track submissions are welcome.

This is an invention sprint, not a product launch. Your solution does not need to be production-ready, but it should demonstrate the problem, intended users, how the solution works, and why it is worth developing further.

Your design should consider real-world conditions such as trust and verification, low bandwidth, accessibility, privacy, multilingual access, local relevance, and clear next steps for users.

Your project idea must be your own. AI tools may support development, but should not generate your core capstone idea.

### Submission process
Your submission should have these five things:

A working proof of concept

A GitHub repository

A short demo video

A pitch deck

A written summary

All work must be original, created for this hackathon, and accessible to the judging team.
